A Mahwah patio company.

Patio Pro's designs and builds paver patios and the outdoor work that usually goes with them.

The work is outdoor: patios, walls, fire pits, kitchens, lighting, and the repairs and extensions that keep a yard usable. Yards here deal with freeze-thaw, wet springs, and slopes that run toward the house. Those conditions belong in the estimate, next to the paver and the shape of the patio.

An estimate starts with the town, a look at the yard, and a written scope. If a project needs a licensed trade — gas, electric, or an engineer's design for a tall wall — that is named in the conversation instead of being folded into a vague patio price.

  1. Scope in writing

    Materials, edges, and what is not included are stated before work is scheduled.

  2. Base and drainage first

    The part you do not see is discussed before the part you do.

  3. Trades called out

    Gas, electric, and engineering are identified when a patio crew should not be doing them.

  4. Northern NJ weather

    Freeze, runoff, and shade are part of the layout, not a surprise after the first winter.
