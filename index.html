How the work is judged

Built around the base, not the brochure.

Groundwork first

Base depth, compaction, and where water goes are part of the estimate. A paver color is not a substitute for that.

Scope in writing

Materials, edges, and what is not included are stated before anything is scheduled.

Trades named

Gas, electric, and engineering are called out when a patio crew should not be the one doing them.

Questions

Straight answers.

Does the estimate form send an email?

Not yet. It confirms on the page and lets you copy a summary. No phone number or inbox is connected on this preview.

Where do you work?

Patio Pro's is based in Mahwah and serves northern New Jersey, especially Bergen County and nearby Passaic and Morris towns.

What should I include?

Town, project type, a rough size, and anything about slope, access, or how you want to use the space.

The fire photo is a pizza oven. Do you still build fire pits?

Yes. Fire pits are part of the work. The photo on the site is a wood-fired oven from a Mahwah kitchen, and it is labeled that way.

Who handles gas or electric?

If a kitchen, fire feature, or lighting plan needs a licensed trade, that scope is named in the estimate instead of being assumed.
