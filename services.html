    Paver patios

    A paver patio is the usual starting point: a level surface for a table, a grill, or a path out from the kitchen. Shape, border, and base are planned for northern New Jersey weather — rain that has to go somewhere, and winters that heave a thin base.

    New patios and rebuilds are both in scope, along with steps, landings, and a walkway to the driveway.

    Useful to have ready

    • Town, and which door the patio should meet
    • A rough size
    • Photos of slope, downspouts, and obstacles
    • Dining, grill, or both

    02 · Clifton

    Retaining walls

    Many North Jersey lots drop away from the house or climb toward a neighbor. A wall is how a slope becomes a flat patio, a bed, or a safer walk.

    Drainage and what sits above the wall matter as much as the face. Tall walls, or walls holding a driveway, may need an engineer. That is flagged before a color is chosen.

    Useful to have ready

    • Approximate height of the slope
    • What is above and below the wall
    • A side photo of the grade
    • Whether you want steps in the wall

    03 · Shown: pizza oven, Mahwah

    Fire pits

    A fire pit belongs in the patio plan, not dropped on at the end. Seating distance, smoke, and materials change the size of the paved area. The photo above is a wood-fired pizza oven, not a fire pit.

    Wood-burning and gas-ready layouts are both conversations. If gas is part of the idea, it is listed on its own so patio work is not confused with a licensed gas connection.

    Useful to have ready

    • Wood-burning or gas-ready, if you know
    • Where people will sit
    • Distance to the house, fence, and trees
    • Whether this replaces an old stone ring

    04 · Mahwah

    Outdoor kitchens

    An outdoor kitchen can be a grill pad and a counter, or a longer run. Either one needs a stable patio and a clear plan for utilities.

    Patio Pro's builds the hardscape and the masonry counter base. Gas lines and electrical circuits are separate scope when a kitchen needs them.

    Useful to have ready

    • The grill you have, or the size you expect
    • How many people you cook for
    • Where it sits relative to the door
    • Whether you want power or gas later

    05 · Mahwah, at night

    Outdoor lighting

    Path lights, step lights, cap lights, and fixtures over the patio make a yard usable after dinner. The easy time to place them is while the base is open.

    This is the fixture layout that belongs with the hardscape. Wiring that needs a licensed electrician is named in the estimate.

    Useful to have ready

    • Steps, path, patio, or grill
    • Low path lights, brighter patio light, or both
    • Any outlets already on the house
    • A dusk photo, if you have one

    06 · Ramsey

    General patio work

    Not every job is a new patio. Settled pavers, a narrow walk, a crumbling edge, or a patio that should have been wider are general patio work. Walkways and front steps sit here too.

    Sometimes a section is lifted and reset. Sometimes the better scope is a partial rebuild. The estimate says which, and why.

    Useful to have ready

    • What is going wrong, or what you want added
    • Photos after a rain, if water is the issue
    • Repair only, or an extension
    • How people get from the street to the door
